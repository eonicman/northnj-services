Frequently Asked Questions

Is claiming really free? ▼

Yes, 100% free. Your business is already listed — claiming just lets you update the information, add photos, and respond to customers. No credit card, no hidden fees.

How do you verify I own the business? ▼

We'll call the phone number on your listing or send a verification email. If neither works, we'll ask for a business document (utility bill, tax filing, etc.). The whole process usually takes less than a day.

What's the difference between free and Premium? ▼

Free listings show your name, phone, and address. Premium adds featured placement (top of search results), photos, customer reviews, a direct contact form, and click-to-call buttons. See full comparison.

My business info is wrong. Can I fix it? ▼

Yes! That's exactly why claiming is important. Once verified, you can update your hours, phone number, address, services, and description at any time.

I already have a website. Why do I need this? ▼

North NJ Services appears in local Google searches for plumbers, electricians, HVAC, and contractors in northern New Jersey. Claiming your listing adds a link to your website and puts you in front of customers who are actively looking for your services.
